What matters

Their ads never touch the main site &mdash; third competitor in this file doing this

All 94 playbook ads point at scale.gymlaunch.com/playbook. The main site gymlaunch.com carries only GTM and HubSpot — no Meta Pixel at all. The squeeze page carries Meta Pixel, Clarity, HubSpot, Intercom, ClickFunnels and New Relic. Two entirely different stacks. Viral Coach and Brez Scales do the same thing. Three unrelated operators separate the brand site from the paid conversion page; we run one page for both jobs.

94 ads, 3 ideas

Collapsed by copy, the 94 records are only three concepts — and the spread is lopsided: 28 variants of the winner, 11 of the one still testing, 1 of the third. That is Andromeda bundling in the open. They are not writing new ads, they are re-uploading the one that works. The winning concept is a 9-second video.

The subhead disqualifies the desperate on purpose

“For gym owners who are doing ‘ok’ on paper, but haven't seen a real jump in… a long time.” It does not target the gym about to close. It targets the plateaued owner who still has money and has stopped believing anything will change. That is a buyer, not a charity case. worth stealing

The best hook is a fragility argument, not a promise

“If your gym only has one way to get new members, you're one bad month away from a real problem.” Scored 96 / Winning with 28 variants. It sells nothing and promises nothing — it just makes the prospect's current stability feel like luck.

They cannot keep their own page count straight

The same PDF is advertised as “130 plus pages”, “140+ pages” and “137 pages” across three live concepts. Recorded as observed. Either sloppy, or evidence that the number itself is being tested.

The brand is stale in our own registry

The best-performing ad is signed “Mike”, not Alex Hormozi. Hormozi sold Gym Launch; our registry still lists him as the creator. Flagged rather than silently corrected.
